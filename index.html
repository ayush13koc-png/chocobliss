Our story

Born from the cacao pod, shaped for the heart.


Choco Bliss began with a simple belief: chocolate is not a snack, it is a feeling. Every piece is slow-conched from carefully sourced cacao, balanced with spice and silk, and wrapped in gold.

Inspired by the Lover archetype, our brand speaks in warmth, sensuality and indulgence — from the gold-foil wrappers to the aroma of the first unwrapped square.
